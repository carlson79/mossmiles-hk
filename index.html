About

Moss is the microscope; miles lead to the distance. This site keeps four kinds of field notes: the macro ecology of deserts and trails, the micro world of moss and morphology, investment notes where observation becomes judgment, and the nature & humanities shelf where music and poetry live.

Ecology and investing share one discipline: read the system at every scale, then be patient. These notes record one person's footprints on the road to the future — and may they light a small lamp for those feeling lost in the age of AI.
